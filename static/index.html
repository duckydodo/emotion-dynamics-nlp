    Methodological Boundaries & Research Limitations

    1. TV Sitcom Domain Limitation (MELD) MELD is sourced from TV comedic series (Friends). Dialogue features scripted sarcasm, exaggerated emotional reactions, and theatrical banter that may not generalize directly to clinical, business, or casual messaging.
    2. Text-Only Modality Limitation The models process textual utterances only. Vocal acoustic tone, pitch shifts, facial micro-expressions, and body language present in raw audiovisual interactions are not captured.
    3. Categorical 7-Class Taxonomy Human emotion is continuous, multi-layered, and blended. The 7 discrete classes (Anger, Disgust, Fear, Joy, Neutral, Sadness, Surprise) represent a simplified discrete modeling choice.
    4. Uncalibrated Softmax Confidence Softmax confidence percentages indicate normalized relative logit margins, not calibrated Bayesian posterior probabilities of psychological truth.
    ⚠️ Research note: Non-Clinical Use & Ethical Safeguards This software is designed exclusively for natural language processing research, conversational dynamics analysis, and computational linguistics. It must never be used for mental health diagnoses, emotional profiling, employee surveillance, or autonomous psychological assessment.
